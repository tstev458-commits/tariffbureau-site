Overview

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

The Tariff Bureau LLC ("The Tariff Bureau," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our website at tariffbureau.com, interact with TariffIQ™, or engage with our advisory services.

By using our website or services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use our services.

Important: The Tariff Bureau is an advisory firm. We are not a law firm and do not provide legal advice. Nothing in our communications or services constitutes legal, tax, or customs brokerage advice. Clients are solely responsible for all decisions made in reliance on our advisory services.

Section 1

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us and information collected automatically when you use our website or services.

Information You Provide

  • Name, email address, phone number, and company name submitted through intake forms or contact forms
  • Product descriptions, supplier information, country of origin, and import volume data submitted for advisory review
  • Business documentation, entry summaries, or supporting materials you choose to share
  • Communications you send us via email, the TariffIQ chatbot, or contact forms
  • Payment information collected through our authorized payment processors (we do not store raw payment card data)

Information Collected Automatically

  • Log data including IP address, browser type, pages visited, time and date of visits, and referring URLs
  • Device information including operating system, browser version, and screen resolution
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies as described in our Cookie section below
  • TariffIQ chatbot interaction data including queries submitted and responses generated
Section 2

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and improve our advisory services and TariffIQ platform
  • To review intake submissions and deliver structured advisory assessments
  • To communicate with you about your engagement, service status, and follow-up items
  • To process payments and maintain billing records in connection with signed engagement letters
  • To send regulatory updates, CAPE enrollment alerts, and trade intelligence relevant to your engagement
  • To analyze website usage and improve the performance and content of our platform
  • To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal obligations
  • To protect the rights, property, and safety of The Tariff Bureau, our clients, and the public

We will not use your business documentation or import data for any purpose other than delivering the services you have engaged us for, and we will not sell or license your proprietary business information to third parties.

Section 3

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted vendors who assist us in operating our website, processing payments, or delivering services — all bound by confidentiality obligations
  • AI Platform: TariffIQ is powered by Anthropic's Claude API. Queries submitted through the TariffIQ chatbot are processed by Anthropic in accordance with their privacy policy and data use terms
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or governmental authority, or when necessary to protect our rights or the safety of others
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or a portion of our assets, with appropriate confidentiality protections
  • With Your Consent: In any other circumstance where you have provided explicit written consent
Section 4

TariffIQ Chatbot & AI Processing

The TariffIQ chatbot on our website is powered by Anthropic's Claude AI. When you submit queries through TariffIQ, your input is transmitted to Anthropic's API for processing. Please be aware of the following:

  • Do not submit sensitive personal information, Social Security numbers, financial account numbers, or confidential trade secrets through the chatbot
  • TariffIQ is designed for general IEEPA tariff and CAPE advisory questions — not for transmitting proprietary business documents
  • Chatbot conversations are not attorney-client privileged communications
  • Anthropic's own privacy policy governs their processing of API data — we encourage you to review it at anthropic.com

For sensitive business information, please use our secure intake form or contact us directly at legal@tariffbureau.com.

Section 5

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience and analyze site usage. Types of cookies we may use include:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function properly
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences across visits

You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website. We do not use cookies to serve targeted advertising.

Section 6

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, maintain our engagement records, and comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations. Specifically:

  • Client engagement files are retained for a minimum of seven (7) years following completion of the engagement, consistent with applicable recordkeeping standards
  • Website analytics data is retained for up to twenty-six (26) months
  • Contact form and general inquiry data is retained for up to three (3) years if no engagement results
  • Chatbot interaction logs may be retained by Anthropic in accordance with their data retention policies
Section 7

Data Security

We implement commercially reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our security measures include:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data transmitted through our website
  • Access controls limiting employee access to client information on a need-to-know basis
  • Vendor security assessments for third-party service providers with access to client data
  • Cloudflare Web Application Firewall (WAF) protection for our website infrastructure

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Section 8

Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention obligations
  • Opt-Out: Opt out of non-essential marketing communications at any time by contacting us or using unsubscribe links in emails
  • Data Portability: Request a portable copy of your information in a common format where technically feasible

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at legal@tariffbureau.com. We will respond to verifiable requests within thirty (30) days.

Section 9

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, including CBP.gov, the ACE Secure Data Portal, and other government or trade resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

Section 10

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for business professionals and are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. We will post the updated policy on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of our website or services after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

For material changes affecting how we use client engagement data, we will provide direct notice to active clients via email.
