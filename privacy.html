Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 10, 2026  ·  The Tariff Bureau LLC  ·  Atlanta, Georgia

The Tariff Bureau LLC (“The Tariff Bureau,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) operates the website tariffbureau.com and the TariffIQ™ advisory platform. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information we receive from visitors to our website and clients who use our services.

By using our website or services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you submit an intake form, contact us, or engage our services, we may collect:

  • Name, title, and contact information (email, phone)
  • Company name, address, and tax identification number (EIN)
  • Import data including CBP entry numbers, HTS codes, country of origin, and duty amounts
  • Customs broker information
  • ACE portal status and compliance history
  • Any additional information you provide in intake forms or correspondence

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain information including:

  • IP address and browser type
  • Pages visited, time on site, and referring URLs
  • Device type and operating system
  • Analytics data via Google Analytics (GA4) and Cloudflare Web Analytics

AI Interaction Data

When you interact with the TariffIQ™ chatbot (Alex Monroe), your conversation inputs are transmitted to Anthropic’s Claude API for processing. We do not store raw conversation transcripts beyond the session unless you submit a formal intake. Anthropic’s privacy practices are governed by their own Privacy Policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide IEEPA tariff refund advisory services and eligibility assessments
  • Send DocuSign engagement documents (NDA, Engagement Letter, Terms of Service)
  • Communicate with you about your engagement and service delivery
  • Generate invoices and process payments via QuickBooks Online
  • Improve our website, TariffIQ platform, and advisory services
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations
  • Protect against fraudulent or unauthorized activity

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Service providers — including DocuSign (electronic signatures), QuickBooks Online (invoicing), Formspree (form processing), Vercel (hosting), and Cloudflare (security and CDN)
  • Licensed customs brokers — only when required to facilitate CAPE Declaration filings on your behalf, and only with your explicit consent
  • Legal counsel — when necessary to protect our rights or comply with legal process
  • Regulatory authorities — if required by law, court order, or government request

All service providers are bound by confidentiality obligations and are prohibited from using your information for any purpose other than providing services to us.

4. Data Retention

We retain client engagement data for a minimum of seven (7) years from the date of engagement, consistent with standard business record-keeping practices and applicable tax and customs regulations. Intake form submissions are retained for the duration of the engagement plus three (3) years. You may request deletion of your data subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

5. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including SSL/TLS encryption, Cloudflare WAF protection, and access controls on our systems. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information.

6. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies for analytics and site functionality. We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand site usage and Cloudflare Web Analytics for performance monitoring. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect site functionality.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to retention obligations)
  • Opt out of marketing communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at legal@tariffbureau.com.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

9. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of our website or services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
