Taiwan's Export Profile and IEEPA Impact

Taiwan is the global leader in semiconductor fabrication and a major exporter of printed circuit boards, electronic components, and precision machinery to the United States. IEEPA tariffs on Taiwan-origin goods applied to a product set with high per-unit value — meaning that even moderate import volumes generated substantial duty payments. U.S. importers of Taiwan-origin electronics and components should evaluate their IEEPA exposure carefully.

Semiconductors & ICs

High-value, high-volume imports. Even modest IEEPA rates on semiconductor shipments generate significant per-entry duty amounts.

PCBs & Components

Printed circuit boards and electronic assemblies from Taiwan are a primary procurement category for U.S. electronics manufacturers.

Precision Machinery

CNC equipment, tooling, and precision instruments from Taiwan's industrial sector. Evaluate HTS chapters 84 and 90.

CHIPS Act Intersection

U.S. companies investing in domestic semiconductor manufacturing under the CHIPS Act who also imported Taiwan-origin components during the IEEPA period face a complex but significant recovery opportunity. IEEPA duty refunds on component imports can partially offset the transition costs of supply chain restructuring. Our team can analyze the full picture for CHIPS-recipient companies.
