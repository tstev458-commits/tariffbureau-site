About The Tariff Bureau

Built for one moment
in trade history.

The Tariff Bureau LLC is an Atlanta-based AI-powered advisory firm focused exclusively on IEEPA tariff refund recovery and trade intelligence — launched in April 2026 to serve U.S. importers and DDP foreign manufacturers navigating the CBP CAPE portal.

Our Mission

Close the gap between
eligible importers and their refunds.

"The Tariff Bureau exists because 92% of eligible U.S. importers have not yet enrolled in the CBP CAPE portal — despite a $166 billion refund pool and a June 7 appeal deadline. We exist to close that gap."

In February 2026, the U.S. Supreme Court ruled in Learning Resources Inc. v. Trump that all IEEPA tariffs imposed between April 5, 2025 and February 24, 2026 were unlawfully levied. CBP launched the CAPE portal on April 20, 2026 to process refund claims. The opportunity is real, time-limited, and largely unclaimed.

The Tariff Bureau was incorporated in Atlanta, Georgia on April 10, 2026 — specifically to serve this moment. We provide structured advisory from initial eligibility screening through full CAPE claim recovery, powered by TariffIQ™, our proprietary AI trade intelligence platform.

What We Do

Six advisory tiers.
One focus.

Every service The Tariff Bureau provides is oriented around a single outcome: helping U.S. importers and eligible DDP foreign manufacturers recover IEEPA tariff overpayments and navigate trade compliance risk with clarity and confidence.

Our advisory spans the full lifecycle — from a $1,500 flat eligibility screening for importers not yet sure they qualify, to end-to-end Full Recovery Package engagements, Claim Audit & Optimization for large claims facing False Claims Act risk, Rerouting Analysis for ongoing supply chain restructuring, and Monitoring Retainer services for active importers requiring continuous trade intelligence.

All advisory is delivered through TariffIQ™ — our AI trade intelligence platform — and reviewed by Alex Monroe, our Senior Trade Intelligence Analyst persona built on Anthropic’s Claude API.

Who We Serve

Mid-market importers.
DDP manufacturers. Lenders.

The Tariff Bureau serves three primary client segments. First, U.S. importers and CFOs who paid IEEPA tariffs between April 2025 and February 2026 and have not yet filed a CAPE Declaration. Second, DDP foreign manufacturers from Vietnam, Taiwan, South Korea, India, Bangladesh, and Indonesia who absorbed IEEPA tariff costs into their pricing and margins. Third, lenders and collateral valuation teams who need independent tariff refund claim assessments for financing decisions.

We require a minimum estimated recovery of $50,000 for Full Recovery Package engagements. The $1,500 Eligibility Screening is available to any importer evaluating their position.
