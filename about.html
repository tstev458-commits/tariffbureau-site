Why We Exist

In February 2026, the U.S. Supreme Court ruled in Learning Resources Inc. v. Trump that IEEPA-based tariffs were an unconstitutional overreach of executive authority. The decision opened an estimated $100 billion to $175 billion in recoverable tariff payments through CBP's CAPE portal.

The problem was immediate: most eligible importers had no idea the ruling happened. Fewer understood the CAPE filing process. And the vast majority — over 92% of the 330,000 eligible importers — had never enrolled in the ACE/ACH electronic payment system required for CBP to actually pay them.

The Tariff Bureau was formed on April 10, 2026, with a single mandate: close that gap. We built TariffIQ — powered by Alex Monroe — to bring enterprise-grade trade intelligence directly to the mid-market importers and international DDP manufacturers who have the most to recover and the least institutional support to do it.

This is not a long-term consulting engagement. It is a precise, time-sensitive recovery operation. We move fast, we file correctly, and we take our fee from the refund — never from your operating budget.

The Legal Basis

Learning Resources Inc. v. Trump — February 2026

⚖️
The Ruling
The Supreme Court held that IEEPA tariffs levied by executive action — including Section 301 China tariffs and the April 2025 "Liberation Day" schedule — exceeded the statutory authority granted by Congress and were constitutionally invalid.
🏛️
The Mechanism
CBP opened the CAPE (Claims for Administrative Processing and Entry) portal to receive refund declarations. Importers with valid ACE/ACH enrollment who file correctly are entitled to direct ACH reimbursement of overpaid duties.
⏱️
The Window
Refund claims operate under statutory timelines. Protest and reliquidation deadlines are strict and non-extendable. Importers who delay risk permanently forfeiting recoverable balances. Speed and accuracy are not optional — they are the strategy.
