Why We Exist

In February 2026, the U.S. Supreme Court ruled that IEEPA-based tariffs exceeded the statutory authority granted by Congress and were constitutionally invalid. The decision opened an estimated $100 billion to $175 billion in recoverable tariff payments through CBP's CAPE portal.

The problem was immediate: most eligible importers had no idea the ruling happened. Fewer understood the CAPE filing process. And the vast majority — over 92% of the 330,000 eligible importers — had never enrolled in the ACE/ACH electronic payment system required for CBP to actually pay them.

The Tariff Bureau was formed on April 10, 2026, with a single mandate: close that gap. We built TariffIQ — powered by Alex Monroe — to bring enterprise-grade trade intelligence directly to the mid-market importers and international DDP manufacturers who have the most to recover and the least institutional support to do it.

This is not a long-term consulting engagement. It is a precise, time-sensitive recovery operation. We move fast, we file correctly, and we take our fee from the refund — never from your operating budget.

The Legal Basis

Supreme Court IEEPA Ruling — February 2026

⚖️
The Ruling
The Supreme Court held that IEEPA tariffs levied by executive action exceeded the statutory authority granted by Congress and were constitutionally invalid — opening the door to refund recovery for approximately 330,000 importers.
🏛️
The Mechanism
CBP opened the CAPE portal on April 20, 2026 to receive refund declarations. Importers with valid ACE/ACH enrollment who file correctly are entitled to direct ACH reimbursement. First payments began issuing May 12, 2026.
⏱️
The Window
Refund claims operate under strict statutory timelines. Phase 1 covers unliquidated entries and entries within 80 days of liquidation. Importers who delay risk permanently forfeiting recoverable balances. Speed and accuracy are the strategy.
