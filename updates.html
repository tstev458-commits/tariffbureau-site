ACH Bank Data Missing — 1,880+ Refunds Stuck at CBP

CBP has confirmed that approximately 1,880 consolidated refunds are currently blocked because importers failed to register ACH bank account information in their ACE Portal accounts. These refunds are approved and ready to pay — but CBP cannot issue them without a valid ACH destination.

If you have an active CAPE Declaration and have not confirmed your ACH info in ACE, this could be your claim. Check your ACE account importer sub-account immediately.

April 20, 2026
Launch

CBP CAPE Portal Goes Live — Phase 1 Open for Submissions

At 8:00 AM EST on April 20, 2026, CBP activated Phase 1 of the CAPE tool within the ACE Secure Data Portal. Importers of record and their authorized customs brokers can now submit IEEPA tariff refund requests electronically via CSV upload (CAPE Declaration). Phase 1 is limited to unliquidated entries and entries within 80 days of liquidation.

This follows the U.S. Supreme Court's February 2026 ruling affecting an estimated 330,000 importers and $166 billion in duties across 53 million entries.
